About

Damien Boykin is an Austin-based actor who works across Shakespeare, contemporary drama and comedy.

He trained at the Stella Adler Studio of Acting’s three-year professional conservatory in New York. Before that he studied improv at iO and the Annoyance Theatre in Chicago while performing around the city.

His stage work includes Hector MacQueen in Murder on the Orient Express at ZACH Theatre and James McDermott in Alias Grace at Cincinnati Playhouse, along with touring productions of Cyrano and The Jungle Book. He also writes and produces his own comedy, including the one-person show E=MC2.
